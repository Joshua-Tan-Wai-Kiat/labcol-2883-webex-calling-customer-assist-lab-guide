## Step 1: Login to Control Hub {#step-1-login-to-control-hub}
<ol type="1">
<li><p>Launch Chrome browser from Workstation 1</p></li>
<li><p>Click on “Cisco Webex Links” &gt; note the username and password
shown under “Cisco Webex Control Hub” – or you can refer to your note
from Task 1 – Accessing your Lab.</p></li>
</ol>

## Step 2: Change Control Hub idle timeout {#step-2-change-control-hub-idle-timeout}
<ol type="1">
<li><p>Under Management, click on “Organization Settings”</p></li>
<li><p>Scroll down until you can see “Control Hub’s idle
timeout”</p></li>
<li><p>Change the value from 20 minutes (Default) to “4 hours”</p></li>
</ol>
<figure class="lab-figure"><a aria-label="Enlarge Lab guide screenshot 28" class="screenshot-link" href="assets/images/image28.png"><img alt="Lab guide screenshot 28" class="lab-screenshot" height="902" loading="lazy" src="assets/images/image28.png" width="2226"/></a></figure>
<ol start="4" type="1">
<li><p>Click “Save”</p></li>
</ol>

<p>If your <strong>monitor's</strong> limited resolution is at or
smaller than 1440x900, you will need to zoom out to 80% (Ctrl + “-“) to
better view the screen content for the rest of this lab.</p>
